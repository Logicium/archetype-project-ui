# Apotome — Photo Guide

Great photos make every site feel premium. This guide works for any small business — restaurants, retail, services, studios, makers, or trades.

## Where do photos go?

Send your photographer the **filenames** below and drop the final, web-optimized JPGs into your site's `public/photos/` folder (or upload them in the admin if your site supports uploads).

## Variants

| Variant      | Total photos | Gallery shots |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------- |
| Essentials   | ~8           | 6             |
| Portfolio    | ~16          | 12            |

You can start with **Essentials** today and upgrade to **Portfolio** later — the site will automatically use the larger set when the files appear.

## Required (Essentials)

| Filename            | Shot                                                     |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `hero.jpg`          | Signature image of your business, golden hour            |
| `about-room.jpg`    | Wide interior showing the space at its best              |
| `feature-01.jpg`    | Hero product, dish, service, or piece of work            |
| `feature-02.jpg`    | Secondary product/service, 45° angle                     |
| `feature-03.jpg`    | A third highlight — variety shows range                  |
| `interior-01.jpg`   | A defining detail (counter, workbench, display, bar)     |
| `interior-02.jpg`   | A window or doorway with daylight                        |
| `team-01.jpg`       | You or a team member at work — hands, focus, craft       |

## Don't have a storefront?

Replace `exterior-01.jpg` with an environment that represents your work:

- **Mobile / on-site services** — a clean truck, gear laid out, a job in progress
- **Online businesses** — your desk, studio, packaging station, or workshop
- **Home-based** — the corner of your home you've made a craft of

## Photo specs

- **2400 px** on the long edge minimum
- Color-graded **JPG, sRGB, quality ~80**
- **No watermarks** or logos burned in
- **Horizontal** where possible — the gallery handles tall shots automatically
- Keep files under ~600 KB each when possible

## Tips that always work

- Shoot during the **golden hour** (1 hour after sunrise / before sunset)
- Use **available light**; avoid on-camera flash
- Capture **hands and motion** — pouring, plating, building, packing, fitting
- Keep your **brand colors consistent** across the set
- Take 5–10 frames of every shot — pick the best one later
- People in the frame should be **doing the work**, not posing for the camera

## What to avoid

- Heavy filters or trendy color grades that will look dated in a year
- Cluttered backgrounds — clear the bench, hide the box of supplies
- Stock photos. They always look like stock photos.
- Phone photos with HDR / Beauty modes turned on — turn them off
